Privacy

Last updated July 2026

No accounts. Tractform does not require sign-in and does not run a user database for your plans.

Location stays on your device. City, state, ZIP, and hardiness zone preferences are stored in your browser’s localStorage so they can persist across pages. Clearing site data in your browser removes them.

Third-party lookups. When you enter a city, state, or ZIP, the site may request public lookup services to resolve a ZIP code or USDA hardiness zone (for example Zippopotam.us and phzmapi.org). Those requests include the place or ZIP you typed so the service can return a result. We do not control those services’ independent practices.

Hosting. The site is served as static files (currently via Vercel). Standard web server and CDN logs may record IP address, user agent, and requested URLs as part of normal delivery and security.

No advertising cookies today. We do not currently use marketing pixels or ad networks. If analytics or accounts are added later, this page will be updated and any required consent will be introduced then.
